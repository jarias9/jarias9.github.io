Selected HPC work.

01

Architecture & technical leadership

I lead a five-person engineering team modernizing a production scientific codebase maintained by 50+ developers. I designed its CPU/GPU portability API for memory, kernel execution, and math libraries, and guide sponsor and contractor plans for architecture, infrastructure, and incremental Kokkos/Umpire adoption.

02

Profiling & optimization

Loop fusion and improved memory efficiency delivered a 1.98× speedup for a routine in production code, with up to 8× speedup in its microbenchmark. My optimizations also include a 10% faster OpenMP submodule with bitwise numerical agreement. I contributed numerical vectorization to a team effort that delivered a 20% code-wide speedup.

I built a Caliper/Adiak/PAPI profiling library for MPI+GPU and multi-phase MPMD applications. My roofline analyses and memory studies informed performance requirements and acceptance criteria. I also resolved thread races and MPI/pthread shutdown deadlocks.

Instrumentation across CUDA · oneAPI · GCC

03

Numerical libraries

I proposed and led an MPI meshfree PDE platform using radial basis functions, securing two internal awards totaling $60K. Its stencil-weight calculation achieved 50–350× speedup on NVIDIA A100 versus AMD EPYC 7413. A separate stencil-application kernel achieved >200× speedup.

I implement selected BLAS/LAPACK routines for Kokkos Kernels Extension and manage contributors, scope, and integration, with automated Serial/OpenMP tests on Intel and Arm and a Grace Hopper CI runner. For LLNL’s BOBA tensor-train library, I implemented batched-SVD paths across CPUs, GPUs, and MPI, with distributed tests in multi-machine Slurm CI.

Eigen/OpenMP · CUDA/cuSOLVER · MPI
RAJA · Umpire · Slurm CI
