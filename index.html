Selected HPC work.

01

Architecture & technical leadership

I lead CPU/GPU modernization of a production scientific codebase maintained by 50+ developers, directing a five-person engineering team. I designed its portability API for memory management, kernel execution, and math libraries. I also lead architecture and infrastructure planning with sponsors and contractors, including the phased adoption of Kokkos and Umpire.

02

Profiling & optimization

I made a production routine run 1.98× faster on CPU by fusing loops and improving memory efficiency. A CPU microbenchmark of the same routine showed up to 8× speedup. I replaced legacy, hand-written loop parallelism built on pthreads with OpenMP, making the submodule 10% faster while preserving bitwise-identical results. I led the vectorization of numerical routines that delivered a 20% code-wide CPU speedup.

I built a profiling library with Caliper, Adiak, and PAPI for MPI applications, including GPU and multi-phase MPMD workloads. My roofline analyses and memory studies informed performance requirements and acceptance criteria. I also resolved race conditions and MPI/pthread shutdown deadlocks.

Supported toolchains: CUDA · Intel oneAPI · GCC

03

Numerical libraries

I implement selected BLAS/LAPACK routines for GTRI’s Kokkos Kernels Extension library, define its scope, set priorities for contributors, and oversee integration. I built automated Serial/OpenMP tests on Intel and Arm and a Grace Hopper CI runner.

For LLNL’s BOBA tensor-train library, I implemented batched SVD on CPUs and GPUs. I integrated CPU, GPU, and distributed tests into multi-machine Slurm CI.

I proposed and led development of a meshfree PDE platform using MPI and radial basis functions, securing $60K in research funding through two internal awards. Stencil-weight calculations ran 50–350× faster on NVIDIA A100 than on AMD EPYC 7413. A separate stencil-application kernel achieved a >200× speedup.

BOBA: Eigen/OpenMP · CUDA/cuSOLVER
RAJA · Umpire
